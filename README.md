### 🔬 Clinical Laboratory Competencies & Diagnostic Disciplines

* **Diagnostic Hematology & Hemostasis:** Automated cell counters (HORIBA ABX Micros), manual 100-cell differentials, leukocyte inclusions, leukemia morphologic review, reticulocyte counts, ESR, and automated coagulation pathways.
* **Immunohematology (Transfusion Medicine):** Tube, solid phase (Werfen SPRCA/Capture), and Ortho MTS Anti-IgG gel card systems for ABO/Rh typing, discrepancy resolution, manual antibody panels, crossmatching, and fetal screens.
* **Clinical Chemistry & Urinalysis:** Automated photometric and ISE platforms (ACE Axcel, Siemens Clinitek), Sebia SPEP, manual microscopic sediment analysis, and chemical fixative compounding.
* **Diagnostic Microbiology & Mycology:** Specimen culture isolation, selective/differential media evaluation, Gram & AFB stains, automated VITEK 2 ID/AST, Kirby-Bauer susceptibility, and identification of anaerobic bacteria, fungi, and parasites.
* **Molecular Diagnostics & Histology:** Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) genotyping workflows, agarose gel electrophoresis, tissue processing, microtomy, and specialized staining.

### 📊 Clinical Informatics, Quality Assurance & Research

* **Quality Control & Regulatory Standards:** Evaluation of Levey-Jennings charts, application of Westgard multi-rules ($1_{2s}, 1_{3s}, 2_{2s}, R_{4s}, 4_{1s}, 10_x$), out-of-control run resolution, and CAP/CLIA compliance.
* **Epidemiological & Diagnostic Data Analysis:** Longitudinal clinical database management (600+ HIV-positive patients, 4,000+ diagnostic test results), data validation, and univariate/bivariate statistical modeling.
* **Statistical Computing & Tooling:** Biostatistical analysis in Jamovi (R-based computing), IBM SPSS 21, and advanced spreadsheet modeling.
* **Flow Cytometry Principles:** Multi-parameter cell population gating, panel design, fluorophore compensation, and immunophenotyping analysis *(Audie L. Murphy VA Hospital Clinical Practicum Track)*.
